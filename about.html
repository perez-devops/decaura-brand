About Decaura

Crafting timeless interiors since 2014

Our Story

Creating Spaces That Tell Stories

Founded in 2014 by acclaimed designer Isabelle Laurent, Decaura has grown from a boutique studio into one of the most sought-after interior design firms in North America. Our name—French for "Black Workshop"—reflects our commitment to timeless elegance and meticulous craftsmanship.

What began as a passion project in a small Manhattan studio has evolved into a full-service design firm with projects spanning residential, commercial, and hospitality spaces across the continent. Yet our core philosophy remains unchanged: every space should tell a unique story, blending the client's vision with our expertise in creating environments that feel both extraordinary and intimately personal.

We believe that interior design is more than selecting beautiful objects—it's about understanding how people live, work, and dream. It's about creating harmony between form and function, past and present, minimalism and warmth.
