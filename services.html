Our Services

Comprehensive design solutions tailored to your vision

01

Interior Design

Our comprehensive interior design service covers every aspect of your project, from initial concept development to final installation. We create cohesive, thoughtfully curated spaces that reflect your personal style while incorporating our expertise in layout, lighting, materials, and finishes.

02

Space Planning

Strategic space planning is the foundation of successful interior design. We analyze traffic patterns, functional requirements, and aesthetic goals to create layouts that maximize both beauty and utility. Our expertise ensures every square foot serves a purpose.

03

Custom Furnishing

When off-the-shelf solutions won't do, we design and commission bespoke furniture pieces tailored precisely to your space and needs. Working with master craftsmen and artisans, we create heirloom-quality pieces that are as functional as they are beautiful.

04

Art Curation

Art brings soul to a space. Our curatorial services help you build or refine an art collection that complements your interior while reflecting your personal taste. We work with galleries, artists, and private collectors to source pieces that elevate your environment.

05

Renovation & Remodeling

From subtle refreshes to complete transformations, we manage renovation projects with precision and care. Our team coordinates with contractors, architects, and specialists to ensure your project stays on schedule, on budget, and exceeds expectations.

06

Design Consultation

Not ready for a full-scale project? Our consultation services provide expert guidance on specific design challenges. Whether you need help with a color palette, furniture arrangement, or material selection, we offer focused expertise to help you move forward with confidence.
